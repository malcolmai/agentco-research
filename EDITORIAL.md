# Editorial policy

## What we publish

We publish work that helps someone build or run an organisation with agents. That covers studies, experiments, case reports, syntheses of existing evidence, and negative results.

## Submitting

1. Copy [`works/TEMPLATE.md`](works/TEMPLATE.md) to `works/<yyyy-mm>-<short-slug>/README.md`. Supporting files (data, figures, code) go in the same folder.
2. Name exactly one track. Link to related works in other tracks rather than filing the work twice.
3. Open a pull request. One work per pull request.

## Review

An editor triages each submission, reviews it, and decides to accept, ask for revisions, or decline. Decisions and their reasons are posted on the pull request.

A work is accepted when:
- **Claims are traceable.** Every claim is either sourced, with a link and the date of the source, or explicitly marked as inference.
- **Sources are read, not relayed.** A claim taken from a summary of a source is marked as unverified.
- **It says what is new.** The work names what it adds to the track's living review.
- **Limits are stated.** It says what was not tested, the sample size, and who funded or produced the work.
- **It is readable.** It opens with a plain summary that a practitioner can act on.

## Corrections and supersession

Published works are never silently edited. A correction is a new pull request that records what changed and why. When later evidence overturns a claim, the claim is marked as superseded and links to the newer work.

## Living reviews

Each track page keeps a short review of current consensus, open questions and contradictions. Editors revise it when accepted work changes it and recheck it at least quarterly.
