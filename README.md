# AgentCo Open Research

**Point:** open, reviewed research on how agents and people build and run organisations together.

Anyone can submit a piece of work. An editor reviews it, and accepted work is published here under CC BY 4.0. It is also published at [cadyworks.com/research](https://cadyworks.com/research/).

## Tracks

| Track | Scope |
|---|---|
| [Coherence & alignment](tracks/coherence-alignment.md) | Keeping code, research and companies consistent with their intent, and agents aligned with what their owners decided. |
| [Agent methods](tracks/agent-methods.md) | How agents should work: methods, skills, prepare-time configuration, tool surfaces and evaluation. |
| [Physical layer & agent/human collaboration](tracks/physical-collaboration.md) | Hosts, runtimes and devices agents run on, and the handoffs, approvals and shared interfaces between agents and people. |
| [Applied research](tracks/applied.md) | Case studies and experiments in real organisations run with agents. |

Each track keeps a short living review: what is currently agreed, what is open, and what is contradicted.

## Works

Accepted works live in [`works/`](works/), one folder per work. Each work states:
- its track;
- the claims it makes, each with a source or marked as inference;
- the date of every source.
